Process Capability Calculator

Enter spec limits and your process stats (or paste raw sample data) to get Cp, Cpk, and an estimated sigma level.

Specification limits

Process data

Mean and standard deviation (sample, n-1) are computed automatically.
Cp measures whether the spread of your process could fit within spec if centered perfectly. Cpk accounts for how centered it actually is — it's always ≤ Cp. As a rule of thumb: below 1.0 means the process is not capable of meeting spec; 1.33 is a common minimum target; 1.67+ is considered excellent (roughly Six Sigma quality when centered).
