Takt Time Calculator

The pace your process needs to run at to match customer demand — no diagram required.

Inputs

—
takt time (time available per unit of demand)
Takt time
Your cycle time
Takt time = available working time ÷ customer demand. It's the rhythm your process needs to hit — not a target for any single station, but the pace the whole line needs to keep up with so you neither over- nor under-produce. If your cycle time is above takt, you can't keep up with demand. If it's well below, you likely have idle capacity or excess staffing on that step.
